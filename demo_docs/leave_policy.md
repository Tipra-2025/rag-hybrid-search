# Leave Policy

## Overview

Acme Corporation's Leave Policy outlines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility requirements, and procedures for requesting time off. Our goal is to support employee wellbeing while maintaining operational continuity.

## Annual Leave (Vacation)

Full-time employees accrue annual leave at the following rates:

| Years of Service | Accrual Rate | Maximum Days Per Year |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 days | 15 days |
| 3–5 years | 18 days | 18 days |
| 6–10 years | 22 days | 22 days |
| 10+ years | 25 days | 25 days |

Annual leave cannot be carried forward beyond 5 days per calendar year. Unused leave above this limit is forfeited on December 31st each year.

Leave must be requested via the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for durations up to 5 days, and at least 15 business days in advance for durations longer than 5 days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over to the following year and is not paid out on termination.

For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a medical certificate is required and must be submitted to HR within 5 business days of returning to work.

## Public Holidays

Acme observes all gazetted public holidays applicable in the location where the employee works. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed as the holiday.

## Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to:

- **16 weeks** of paid maternity leave at full pay for employees with more than 1 year of service.
- **8 weeks** of paid maternity leave for employees with less than 1 year of service.

Leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected due date. The employee must notify HR in writing at least 8 weeks before the intended start date.

## Paternity / Partner Leave

Employees are entitled to **2 weeks** of paid paternity leave to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption of a child. Notification should be given to HR as early as possible.

## Adoption Leave

Adoption leave mirrors maternity leave provisions. The qualifying event is the date of placement of the child.

## Parental Leave (Unpaid)

After completing 12 months of service, employees may apply for up to 52 weeks of unpaid parental leave. Employment continuity is preserved during this period. Applications must be submitted at least 12 weeks before the intended start date.

## Compassionate Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). For extended family bereavement, up to 3 days may be approved at management discretion.

## Study Leave

Employees pursuing qualifications approved by the company are eligible for up to 5 study days per calendar year, in addition to examination days. Applications must be accompanied by proof of enrollment and approved by the manager and HR.

## Leave Without Pay

Employees may apply for unpaid leave for personal reasons after exhausting paid entitlements. Approval is at management discretion and depends on business requirements.

## How to Apply

1. Log in to the HR portal at hr.acme.com.
2. Navigate to **My Leaves → New Request**.
3. Select leave type, start and end dates.
4. Add a comment if required (mandatory for sick leave after 3 days).
5. Submit for manager approval.

Managers must approve or decline requests within 3 business days.

## Leave During Notice Period

Employees who resign or are terminated may not take annual leave during their notice period unless agreed in writing with HR.
