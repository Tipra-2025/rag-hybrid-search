# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to Acme Corporation

Welcome to Acme Corporation! This handbook provides essential information about our company policies, culture, and expectations. Please read it carefully and refer to it whenever you have questions about employment matters.

## Company Values

Acme Corporation operates on four core values:

1. **Integrity** — We do what we say and say what we mean. Honesty in all interactions, internal and external.
2. **Innovation** — We encourage creative problem-solving and support employees who take thoughtful risks.
3. **Collaboration** — Great results come from teams working together across departments and geographies.
4. **Customer Focus** — Every decision we make should improve the experience for our customers.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information and trade secrets.
- Report conflicts of interest promptly to HR.
- Avoid discrimination or harassment of any kind. Acme has zero tolerance for such behavior.
- Use company resources (equipment, internet, email) for work-related purposes.

Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Flexible working arrangements are available with manager approval. Remote work is permitted up to three days per week for eligible roles.

Overtime must be pre-approved by your manager. Non-exempt employees are compensated for all overtime hours at the applicable statutory rate.

## Probationary Period

New employees serve a 90-day probationary period during which performance is closely evaluated. During this period, either party may terminate employment with one week's notice. Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing.

## Performance Reviews

Performance is reviewed formally twice per year — in June and December. The review considers:

- Achievement of objectives set at the start of the review period.
- Demonstration of company values.
- Feedback from peers and stakeholders.
- Professional development progress.

Annual salary adjustments are linked to performance outcomes and budget availability.

## Grievance Procedure

Employees with workplace concerns should:

1. Raise the matter informally with their direct manager.
2. If unresolved, escalate to HR within 10 business days.
3. HR will investigate and respond within 15 business days.
4. If still unresolved, employees may escalate to the Chief People Officer.

All grievances are treated confidentially. Retaliation against an employee who raises a grievance in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## Health and Safety

Acme is committed to maintaining a safe working environment. Employees must:

- Follow all safety procedures posted in the workplace.
- Report hazards or accidents to Facilities immediately.
- Participate in mandatory safety training.
- Not report to work under the influence of alcohol or controlled substances.

## Equipment and Technology

Company-issued equipment must be used primarily for business purposes. The IT team is responsible for all hardware and software procurement. Installing unauthorized software is not permitted. All company data must be stored on approved systems — do not store work data on personal devices or unapproved cloud services.
