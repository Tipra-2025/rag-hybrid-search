# Travel Policy

## Purpose

This Travel Policy provides guidelines for business travel arrangements, reimbursement, and conduct. It applies to all Acme Corporation employees traveling on company business.

## Booking Travel

All business travel must be booked through the company's travel management platform at travel.acme.com or through the Corporate Travel team at travel@acme.com.

**Do not book travel on personal accounts or external sites** — this makes expense tracking harder and may mean you don't receive company-negotiated rates.

Trips must be approved by your direct manager before booking. For trips over $3,000 total cost, VP approval is also required.

## Air Travel

- **Domestic flights**: Economy class for all flights.
- **International flights under 8 hours**: Economy class.
- **International flights 8 hours or longer**: Premium economy is permitted. Business class requires written approval from the Chief People Officer.
- **Booking window**: Book at least 14 days in advance when possible to secure better fares. Bookings within 7 days require manager justification.
- **Loyalty programs**: Employees may retain personal frequent flyer points accrued on company-paid flights.

## Hotels

- Use company-preferred hotel partners where available (listed in the travel platform).
- Standard maximum rates: $200 USD per night (domestic), $250 USD per night (international).
- Major financial centers and conference cities: $300 USD per night — pre-approved by Finance.
- Fees: Room service, minibar, and in-room entertainment are personal expenses. Hotel gym, internet, and laundry for stays over 5 nights are reimbursable.
- Always book **non-refundable** rates only if travel plans are certain; otherwise book refundable rates.

## Ground Transportation

- **Airport transfers**: Ride-share (Uber, Lyft) or public transit preferred. Taxis are acceptable.
- **Car rental**: Required only when no suitable public or ride-share option is available. Compact or economy class. Always purchase the collision damage waiver (CDW) — the company card does not cover rental car insurance.
- **Personal vehicle**: Reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate. Parking and tolls reimbursed at actual cost.
- **Train**: Permitted for domestic travel when cost-effective. Standard class only.

## Per Diem Rates (Meals and Incidentals)

When traveling on company business, employees may claim the following per diem rates instead of submitting individual meal receipts:

| Region | Per Diem Rate |
|---|---|
| Domestic (most cities) | $75 USD per day |
| Domestic (NYC, SF, LA, Chicago) | $95 USD per day |
| International (standard) | $85 USD per day |
| International (high cost: London, Tokyo, Zurich) | $120 USD per day |

Per diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidentals. No additional meal receipts are required when claiming per diem. Employees may choose to itemize actual meal receipts instead but cannot mix methods within the same trip.

## International Travel

Employees traveling internationally must:
- Ensure their passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond the return date.
- Obtain any required visas before departure — contact the Travel team for visa support.
- Register their trip with the Travel team so that emergency support can be provided.
- Carry the company travel insurance card (issued by the Travel team upon booking).
- Be aware of and comply with local laws and regulations.
- Notify HR of any travel to destinations on the restricted travel list (updated quarterly).

## Travel Safety

Employee safety is the top priority:
- Avoid travel to destinations rated Level 3 or 4 by government travel advisories without CISO and C-suite approval.
- Use company-approved transportation providers only in high-risk destinations.
- Do not walk alone at night in unfamiliar areas.
- Keep travel documents and valuables secured.
- In an emergency, contact the 24-hour travel assistance line: +1-800-ACME-HELP.

## Trip Cancellations and Changes

- Cancel non-refundable bookings as soon as the trip is no longer required — credits may be retained for future travel.
- Cancellation fees incurred due to genuine business reasons are reimbursable with manager approval.
- Cancellation fees due to personal reasons are the employee's responsibility.

## Combining Business and Personal Travel

Employees may add personal days to a business trip with manager approval. The company will reimburse the cost of travel as if the personal days were not taken — i.e., the employee pays any incremental cost of extending the trip. Personal days do not count against annual leave if taken in conjunction with an authorized business trip, but must be approved in advance.

## Submitting Travel Expenses

Submit all travel expenses within 10 business days of returning. Use the expense system at expenses.acme.com. Attach all original receipts. Expenses submitted more than 30 days after travel may be declined.

## Travel Insurance

The company provides travel insurance for all business travel booked through the travel platform. Coverage includes medical emergencies, trip cancellation, lost luggage, and emergency evacuation. Details are in the travel platform. Personal travel is not covered.

## Frequent Traveler Program

Employees who travel more than 20 nights per year may be eligible for additional comfort allowances (e.g., airport lounge access, premium economy upgrades on long-haul flights). Contact the Travel team to determine eligibility.
