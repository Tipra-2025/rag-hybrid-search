# Expense Policy

## Purpose

This Expense Policy establishes guidelines for employees submitting business expenses. Acme Corporation reimburses reasonable, necessary, and properly documented expenses incurred in the course of conducting legitimate company business.

## General Principles

- Only expenses that are **business-related, reasonable, and necessary** will be reimbursed.
- Employees must always seek the **most cost-effective option** consistent with the needs of the business.
- **Personal expenses** will not be reimbursed, even if incurred during a business trip.
- **Pre-approval is required** for any single expense exceeding $500 USD (or local equivalent).
- All expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of being incurred. Late submissions may be declined.

## Expense Categories and Limits

### Travel

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic flights | Economy class only | Business class requires VP approval |
| International flights >8 hours | Premium economy permitted | Business class requires C-suite approval |
| Hotels | $200/night (major cities: $300) | Book via preferred vendors |
| Ground transport | Actual cost | Prefer public transit or ride-share |
| Car rental | Economy or compact | Insurance must be purchased |
| Mileage (personal vehicle) | IRS standard rate | Log miles in expense system |
| Parking | Actual cost, $30/day max | |

### Meals and Entertainment

| Category | Limit Per Person | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Business meals (client) | $100 | Requires attendee names and business purpose |
| Business meals (internal) | $50 | Requires attendee names |
| Team lunch/dinner (manager-approved) | $75 | Pre-approval required for groups >5 |
| Alcohol | $30 per person | Only with a meal; no standalone bar bills |
| Coffee/snacks (travel days) | $15 | |

Meal expenses must include a **receipt** and **list of attendees with their company affiliation**.

### Technology and Equipment

Employees may not purchase technology (laptops, phones, monitors, peripherals) on personal expense accounts. All technology purchases go through the IT procurement process.

Software subscriptions require IT Security approval before purchase. Approved individual software subscriptions up to $30/month may be expensed.

### Professional Development

- Conference registration fees up to $2,000 per event require manager approval.
- Conference fees above $2,000 require VP approval.
- Training courses, certifications, and books up to $500 per quarter require manager approval.
- Amounts above $500 require HR review.

### Office Supplies

Working-from-home employees are entitled to a one-time home office setup allowance of $500, approved by HR. Ongoing supplies up to $30/month may be expensed.

## Submitting an Expense Report

1. Log in to the expense system at expenses.acme.com.
2. Create a new report and categorize each expense.
3. Attach receipts (photo or scan) for every item over $25.
4. Add a business purpose description for each expense.
5. Submit for manager approval.

Managers must approve within 5 business days. Approved reports are processed for payment in the next payroll cycle.

## Receipts

Receipts are **required** for all expenses over $25. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient. Meals receipts must show itemized food and drink ordered, not just the total.

If a receipt is genuinely lost, submit a missing receipt declaration form, which is subject to manager and finance approval. Habitually missing receipts may result in denial of reimbursement.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following will NOT be reimbursed:
- Personal entertainment (movies, sporting events, personal subscriptions).
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or toll violations.
- Travel upgrades beyond the permitted class.
- Expenses incurred by a family member.
- Alcohol purchased outside a qualifying business meal.
- Luxury or premium hotels or flights when economy or standard options were available.
- Personal grooming, laundry (except on trips longer than 5 days — then $15/day).
- Expenses not submitted within 30 days without prior approval from Finance.

## Travel Advances

Employees expecting significant travel expenses may request a travel advance from Finance at least 10 business days before the trip. Advances must be reconciled within 10 business days of return.

## Currency and Exchange

International expenses should be submitted in the currency paid. The company will reimburse at the mid-market exchange rate on the date of the transaction.

## Audits

Expense reports are subject to random audit. Employees who submit fraudulent or inflated expense claims will face disciplinary action up to and including termination and referral to law enforcement.
